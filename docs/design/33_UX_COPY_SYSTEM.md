## 1. Voice Pillars & Athletic Tone

ArmSphere communicates with the quiet confidence of an Olympic training hall and an international sports federation. We reject generic tech cheerleading, childish emojis, robotic system errors, and corporate bureaucratic jargon.

### The 5 Voice Pillars:
1. **Authoritative**: Institutional integrity. Statements are factual, definitive, and grounded in official federation rules (WAF/IFA standards).
2. **Athletic**: Grounded in the physical reality of grip strength, tendon resilience, chalked tables, and competitive duels.
3. **Concise**: Eliminate every unnecessary syllable. In high-pressure tournament moments, athletes and referees need immediate clarity.
4. **Respectful**: High regard for athlete dedication, referee impartiality, and the sportsmanship of the armwrestling community.
5. **Action-Oriented**: Buttons and prompts begin with concrete physical action verbs ("Lock In Weight", "Accept Bout", "Inspect Bracket"), never passive labels ("Submit", "OK", "Next").

## 2. Anti-AI Copy Rules (The Slop Rejection Rubric)

Generic AI assistants default to bland corporate cheerleading and sycophantic praise. In ArmSphere, the following patterns are strictly banned:

| Banned AI Pattern | Why It Is Rejected | Approved ArmSphere Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Awesome job! You crushed that workout!"* | Cheap artificial cheerleading. Athletes know their own effort. | **"PR Logged: 52.5 kg Pronation. Baseline updated."** |
| *"Oopsie! Something went wrong on our end!"* | Patronizing, childish, hides actual failure. | **"Network sync delayed. 3 score entries queued locally."** |
| *"Are you sure you want to proceed?"* | Lazy generic modal prompt. | **"Forfeit Match? This grants opponent a 3-0 victory."** |
| *"Click here to view more details."* | Weak desktop web relic. | **"View Full Bracket & Standings"** |
| *"Welcome to the app! Let's get started!"* | Zero-signal fluff. | **"Select your primary arm and competition division."** |
| *"Please wait while we fetch your awesome data..."* | Slow, uninformative loading copy. | **"Synchronizing Table 2 Live Telemetry..."** |

## 3. Exhaustive Copy Inventory

### Section A: Action-Verb Button Labels (Direct Physical Commands)
Never use passive nouns ("Submission", "Save", "Done"). Use precise athletic imperatives:

- Tournament Registration: **"Lock In Division Entry ($45)"**
- Weigh-In Clearance: **"Approve & Lock Scale Weight"**
- Challenge Negotiation: **"Slide to Accept Bout"** / **"Decline Duel"**
- Training Log: **"Record Cupping PR"** / **"Save Workout Log"**
- Referee Scorepad: **"Award Corner Pin"** / **"Assess Technical Foul"**
- Profile Updates: **"Update Stance & Grip"**
- Bracket Management: **"Advance Winner to Semis"**
- Table Assignment: **"Call Competitors to Table 3"**
- Search & Filters: **"Apply Division Filters"** / **"Reset All"**
- Media Upload: **"Post Training Clip"**

### Section D: Onboarding Copy Flow (3-Step Athletic Setup)

#### Step 1: Persona Selection
- **Headline**: "Define Your Competition Role"
- **Subhead**: "Select your primary role. You can switch between active athlete and certified referee workspaces anytime in your profile."
- **Options**:
  - `ATHLETE`: "Train, compete in sanctioned divisions, and track your ELO rating."
  - `OFFICIAL / REFEREE`: "Score table bouts, oversee weigh-ins, and manage brackets."
  - `FAN / SPECTATOR`: "Follow live tables, watch match streams, and track regional rivalries."
- **CTA**: `PROCEED TO PROFILE SETUP`

#### Step 2: Physical Pulling Profile
- **Headline**: "Calibrate Your Combat Stance"
- **Subhead**: "Your dominant arm and pulling technique configure your head-to-head match cards and training metrics."
- **Fields**:
  - Dominant Arm: `[RIGHT ARM]`, `[LEFT ARM]`, `[DUAL PULLER]`
  - Primary Technique: `[TOP-ROLL]`, `[HOOK]`, `[PRESS]`, `[OPEN-HAND SLIP]`
  - Competition Weight Class: Select division (e.g. `Senior Men 85kg / 187 lbs`)
- **CTA**: `CONFIRM ATHLETIC STANCE`

#### Step 3: Federation & Club Affiliation
- **Headline**: "Link Your Club & Province"
- **Subhead**: "Connect with your local training hall and regional armwrestling federation."
- **Fields**: Searchable dropdown for Provincial Association and Local Armwrestling Club.
- **CTA**: `ENTER THE ARENA`

### Section F: Destructive Action Confirmations

1. **Forfeit Tournament Bout**:
   - Title: **"Confirm Bout Forfeit?"**
   - Body: "Forfeiting awards an immediate 3-0 victory to your opponent and moves you directly to the Losers Bracket. This action is irreversible."
   - Confirm CTA: `CONFIRM FORFEIT (RED)`
   - Cancel CTA: `RETURN TO BOUT`

2. **Revoke Tournament Sanction**:
   - Title: **"Revoke Official Sanction?"**
   - Body: "Revoking sanction removes this event from the official federation calendar and disqualifies all matches from awarding official ELO rating points."
   - Confirm CTA: `REVOKE SANCTION (RED)`
   - Cancel CTA: `KEEP SANCTIONED`

3. **Delete Training Log Entry**:
   - Title: **"Remove PR Lift?"**
   - Body: "Deleting this 52.5kg cupping entry will recalculate your 30-day baseline strength curve."
   - Confirm CTA: `DELETE ENTRY`
   - Cancel CTA: `KEEP RECORD`

### Section G: Referee Table-Side Scorepad Terminology (WAF / IFA Standard)

- **Elbow Foul**: "Elbow off pad in forward, rear, or lateral direction during pull."
- **Intentional Slip**: "Deliberately slipping grip outside neutral straps in losing position."
- **False Start**: "Early drive before referee 'READY... GO!' cadence."
- **Neutral Strap Applied**: "Grip slipped without foul. Directing strap application by referee."
- **Warning**: "Minor infraction (shoulders squared past legal angle, delay of grip)."
- **Pin Declared**: "Competitor's fingers, wrist, or forearm touched down beyond horizontal pin line."
