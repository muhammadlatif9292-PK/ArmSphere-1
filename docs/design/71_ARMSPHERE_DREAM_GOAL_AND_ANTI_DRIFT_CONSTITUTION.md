## 1. The Preamble & Ultimate Purpose

### What ArmSphere Is NOT:
*   It is **NOT** a vehicle to add more animations.
*   It is **NOT** a showcase to dump more images or video clips.
*   It is **NOT** an imitation of TikTok.
*   It is **NOT** an imitation of the UFC mobile app.
*   It is **NOT** an imitation of the Formula 1 app.
*   It is **NOT** a platform to make every screen flashy or decorative.
*   It is **NOT** a Dribbble concept made real.
*   It is **NOT** an exercise in maximizing the number of UI effects.

### What ArmSphere IS:
**BUILD THE BEST POSSIBLE ARMSPHERE EXPERIENCE FOR ITS ACTUAL PURPOSE.**

ArmSphere must feel like a category-defining competitive armwrestling platform that seamlessly fuses:

## 2. Inputs vs. The Goal

The reference images, deep research findings, TikTok interaction patterns, premium sports products (UFC, NBA, F1), cinematic lighting techniques, Flow-generated media, Flutter animations, and external benchmarks are **INPUTS — NOT THE GOAL**.

External inspiration exists solely to uncover **underlying principles**, which must then be re-imagined as **ArmSphere-specific solutions**.

Every proposal, screen refactor, component design, and pull request must be judged exclusively against the ArmSphere objective.

---

## 3. The 12-Question Supreme Evaluation Rubric

Before accepting, coding, or merging ANY change, feature, animation, or visual token, the team and any AI agent MUST pass the proposal through this 12-point gatekeeper rubric:

1.  **Does this materially improve the ArmSphere experience?**
2.  **Does it make the product more desirable or memorable?**
3.  **Does it improve clarity, interaction, anticipation, identity, or emotional impact?**
4.  **Does it fit armwrestling specifically?**
5.  **Does it preserve federation credibility?**
6.  **Does it improve the experience rather than merely decorate it?**
7.  **Does it remain coherent with the global ArmSphere design language?**
8.  **Does it work on mobile?**
9.  **Does it remain accessible?**
10. **Does it remain performant?**
11. **Is the complexity justified?**
12. **Could the same result be achieved more elegantly?**

> [!CAUTION]
> **THE "LOOKS COOL" VETO RULE:**
> If the primary justification for an addition, animation, or visual element is *"it looks cool"*, **IT MUST BE REJECTED IMMEDIATELY**.
> Novelty for novelty's sake is strictly prohibited.

---

## 4. The Anti-Drift Canons

1.  **Zero Novelty for Novelty’s Sake**: Every micro-interaction and visual plane must serve physical truth, data clarity, or emotional ceremony.
2.  **Zero One-Off Inconsistencies**: No screen may introduce custom buttons, unique curve formulas, or rogue colors that break the global design language.
3.  **Zero Decorative Redesigns**: Existing, working functionality must never be rewritten or re-architected merely to generate visual novelty.
4.  **Zero Research Override Without Direct Proof**: External research recommendations cannot override established ArmSphere operational rules (sub-50ms latency, offline SQLite sync, dual theme contrast) without explicit evidence and documented reconciliation.
5.  **Zero Brand Imitation**: ArmSphere is not a copy of any other platform. It is the international home of armwrestling.

---

## 5. The Desired Final Experience (The North Star Statement)

> **"An athlete opens ArmSphere and immediately feels that this is a serious, beautiful, modern, premium competitive-sport platform built specifically for armwrestling — and every interaction reinforces that feeling."**

All future planning, asset generation, component styling, and code implementation must move relentlessly toward this single objective.

**Anything that does not materially contribute to this objective is secondary.**
